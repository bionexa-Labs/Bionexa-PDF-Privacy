Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026

Developer: Bionexa Labs

Application: Bionexa PDF

Bionexa Labs respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Bionexa PDF accesses, processes, stores, and shares information when you use the application.

Bionexa PDF is a document utility application that provides PDF creation, conversion, compression, scanning, OCR, editing, sharing, and related document tools.

1. Documents, Images and PDF Files

Bionexa PDF allows you to select images, PDFs and other supported files from your device for processing.

Document processing is designed to occur locally on your device unless you intentionally choose a feature that transfers a file to another application or storage provider.

Bionexa Labs does not operate a server that automatically receives your:

  • PDF documents
  • Scanned documents
  • Selected photos or images
  • OCR text
  • Signatures
  • Converted files

Files you create remain on your device or in a location you select unless you explicitly choose to share or back them up.

2. File and Storage Access

The app may ask you to select files using Android system file, photo or content pickers.

Bionexa PDF uses selected files only to perform the action you request, such as:

  • Image to PDF conversion
  • PDF compression
  • PDF merge and split
  • PDF to JPG or PNG conversion
  • Page deletion and reordering
  • Watermarks and page numbering
  • Password protection
  • OCR text extraction
  • PDF to Word conversion
  • Previewing and editing documents

Output files may be stored in your device's Downloads folder, Bionexa PDF folder, or another location selected by you.

3. Camera and Document Scanner

The Document Scanner feature may use your device camera to capture documents.

Camera access is used only when you choose a scanning or camera function. Captured documents may be cropped, enhanced, converted into images, or converted into PDF files.

Bionexa Labs does not remotely activate your camera and does not automatically upload camera captures to Bionexa Labs servers.

4. OCR Text Recognition

Bionexa PDF includes Optical Character Recognition (OCR) features that can extract text from images or documents.

The app uses Google ML Kit technology for supported OCR and document processing functions. ML Kit provides on-device machine-learning capabilities for supported features.

Extracted text is used to provide the function requested by you and may be saved or shared only when you choose to do so.

5. Advertising

Bionexa PDF contains advertisements provided through Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK.

Depending on your device, region, consent choices, and Google's advertising configuration, Google Mobile Ads may process information including:

  • IP address
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • Device or advertising identifiers
  • App interactions such as launches, taps and ad interactions
  • Diagnostic and performance information
  • Information used for advertising and fraud prevention

This information is processed by Google according to Google's own privacy practices.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Google Advertising information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

6. Advertising Consent

Where required, Bionexa PDF may display consent or privacy controls related to advertising.

The availability and behavior of personalized or non-personalized ads may depend on your location, device settings and consent choices.

7. Cloud Backup and External Storage Providers

If you choose Cloud Backup or select a cloud-enabled storage location, your files may be transferred to a storage provider selected by you, such as a provider available through Android's system file interface.

Those files are then subject to the selected provider's own privacy policy and terms.

Bionexa Labs does not automatically upload your documents to a Bionexa Labs cloud server.

8. Sharing Files

When you choose the Share option, Android's sharing system may allow you to send a document to another application, person or service.

Bionexa PDF shares a file only after you initiate the sharing action. Once the file is transferred to another service, that service's privacy practices apply.

9. Feedback and External Applications

If you choose Send Feedback, Rate App, Share App, or another external action, Bionexa PDF may open another application such as your email application, browser, Google Play Store, or Android sharing interface.

Information you voluntarily submit through those third-party applications is governed by their respective privacy policies.

10. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

Bionexa Labs does not require users to create a Bionexa PDF account to use the core PDF tools.

We do not intentionally collect document contents, scanned documents, passwords used to protect PDFs, handwritten signatures, or OCR text on Bionexa Labs servers.

11. Data Security

We use reasonable technical measures designed to protect information handled by the application.

Files processed locally remain subject to the security of your device. Data transmitted by third-party services such as Google Mobile Ads is handled according to the security practices of those services.

12. Data Retention and Deletion

Bionexa PDF does not maintain a Bionexa Labs server-side account containing your processed documents.

Files saved on your device remain there until you delete them.

Files saved to an external or cloud provider remain subject to the retention and deletion controls of that provider.

Temporary files created during document processing may be removed by the app, Android, or when application storage/cache is cleared.

You can remove locally stored Bionexa PDF data by deleting the relevant files or clearing/uninstalling the application, subject to Android's storage behavior.

13. Children's Privacy

Bionexa PDF is a general-purpose productivity and document utility application and is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

14. Third-Party Services

Bionexa PDF may use or interact with:

  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK
  • Google ML Kit
  • Google Play services
  • Android system file, photo and sharing services
  • User-selected storage or cloud providers

Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Bionexa Labs may update this Privacy Policy when app features, technologies, legal requirements, or data practices change.

Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date when appropriate.

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Bionexa PDF's privacy practices, you can contact Bionexa Labs through our public GitHub privacy repository:
